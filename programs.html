CMU'S SAMS

Applicable for: Rising 11th grade students

Duration: Year-round virtual; Summer 6 week in person

Paid?: No, but the application is free

More Information: Not in a professional lab setting and only collaborative project. There is a set curriculum of actual classes. Priority is given to students who are: -raised in a home where English was a second language -currently attend a high school with historically low rates of admittance to top-tier institutions -first-generation college students (parent(s) did not complete a bachelors degree) -eligibile for public assistance programs or free/reduced lunch
